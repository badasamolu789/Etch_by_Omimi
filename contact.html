Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about ETCH

ETCH is a premium creative marketplace where creators can license their work — including scripts, storyboards, music, design, motion, and copy — to buyers around the world. We handle the marketplace, licensing, and discovery so creators can focus on their craft.

Browse the marketplace to find the perfect asset for your project. Click "Request License" on any asset page to start the licensing process. Our team will help you navigate the terms and secure the rights you need.

Sign up for a creator account, complete your profile, and start uploading your creative assets. Our team reviews submissions to ensure quality standards. Once approved, your work becomes available for licensing on the marketplace.

We offer several license types including Exclusive Option, Sync License, and Custom Deal agreements. Each license type has different terms, restrictions, and pricing structures to meet the needs of various projects and budgets.

Creators receive competitive compensation for their work. ETCH takes a small platform fee for facilitating the marketplace, licensing, and secure transactions. Creators set their own prices and receive payouts promptly after successful licensing.
